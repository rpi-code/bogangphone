Before you choose

iPhone Pros & Cons

The strengths and trade-offs of the underlying iPhone platform.

+ The Pros

  • Strong performance on many recent iPhone models.
  • Consistent and polished iOS experience.
  • Good photo and video features on supported models.
  • Useful integration with compatible Apple devices.
  • Security features and software updates for supported devices.

− The Cons

  • Newer iPhones can be expensive.
  • iOS generally offers less system-level customization than Android.
  • Some ecosystem features work best with other Apple products.
  • Out-of-warranty repairs may be costly.
  • Battery, display and camera specifications vary by model.
